chime.wav
---------

nokia.wav
---------

Display name: Nokia
Composition source: The familiar phrase derived from Francisco Tarrega's
1902 solo-guitar work Gran Vals.
Recording: Generated specifically for qmux as a mono, 16-bit PCM rendering
using additive synthesis. No third-party sound recording was used.
